# Current Meta Brief — Patch 26.11 (June 2026)

> Refresh this each patch. Sources: MetaBot.GG, METAsrc, Mobalytics, U.GG, Hotspawn, 1v9 (June 2026).
> Tier lists disagree at the margins — treat this as consensus + what matters at **Gold/Plat**.

## Headline trends
- **Engage-support meta.** Aftershock buffs + enchanter-rune nerfs pushed **engage supports (Rell, Leona, Seraphine, Morgana, Taric)** to the top. ✅ This is our wheelhouse.
- **Tanks/bruisers rule top.** Malphite, Sion, Dr. Mundo, Renekton, Garen. ✅ Also our wheelhouse.
- **Bot-centric fights.** Many comps revolve around the ADC + a hard-engage or peel support.
- At Gold/Plat, **coordination beats mechanics** — engage/teamfight and pick comps overperform vs. soloQ because most enemies don't coordinate disengage.

## Meta Clash comp archetypes (what wins)
1. **Hard-engage teamfight** — Malphite/Rell/Wukong layered engage → 5v5 collapse. *(← our identity)*
2. **Pick/catch** — hooks + lockdown (Vi/Nocturne + Morgana/Thresh) delete a target, take objective.
3. **Protect-the-carry / scaling** — frontline + enchanter peel a hypercarry (Smolder/Xayah + Lulu/Milio). *(we lack enchanters — developmental)*
4. **Poke/siege** — Ezreal/Xerath chip + zone objectives. *(weak fit for our role constraints)*

## Ban-worthy this patch (high-impact, hard to deal with at our elo)
- **Engage we can't peel:** Malphite, Wukong, Rell, Nocturne (blind-side ult).
- **Scaling carries that beat our tempo:** Smolder, Kayle, Veigar (enemy), Master Yi.
- **Vs. our engage specifically:** disengage/peel (Janna, Tahm Kench, Ksante) and Morgana (black shield eats our initiation).
> Default team bans (refine per opponent scout): **Malphite / Master Yi / Smolder** or swap in their known OTP.
