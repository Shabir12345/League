## What's in here
- **`players/`** — per-player profile: rank, role flex, champ pool w/ win rates, strengths, learn-list.
- **`meta/current-patch-meta.md`** — live S-tier per role + OP comps (patch 26.11, refresh each patch).
- **`comps/playbook.md`** — ⭐ ranked team comps built for OUR pools. Start here.
- **`plans/champs-to-learn.md`** — per-player practice priorities over the next month.
- **`draft/clash-day-sheet.md`** — pick-order + ban cheat sheet to keep open during the draft.
- **`data/`** — raw dpm.lol scans (source of truth for the numbers).

## How we use it
1. **Before Clash:** everyone practices their learn-list (`plans/`).
2. **Scouting:** check opponents' op.gg/dpm.lol, note their mains.
3. **Draft day:** open `draft/clash-day-sheet.md`, pick a comp from `comps/playbook.md` that beats what they're likely to run.
4. **After each patch:** I refresh `meta/` and re-check the playbook.

## Team identity (one line)
**Hard-engage teamfight.** We have elite engage-support + control-mid depth. We want to start fights on our terms, collapse on a target, and win the 5v5 — not play slow scaling or poke-siege.
