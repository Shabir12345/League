# 🎯 Clash Day-Sheet — keep this open during the draft

Quick reference for draft + bans. Full detail in `comps/playbook.md`.

**Top** Harendra · **JG** Geeth · **Mid** Steven · **ADC** Shabir · **Sup** Eshantha

## Step 1 — Scout (before draft / day before)
- Check each opponent on dpm.lol or op.gg. Note their **main per role + any one-tricks**.
- Flag: do they have hard engage? a hyperscaler (Smolder/Kayle/Yi)? a Morgana (eats our engage)?

## Step 2 — Bans (team default, adjust to scout)
**Default 3:** `Malphite` · `Master Yi` · `Smolder`
- Swap to ban an **enemy one-trick** if scouting shows one.
- ⚠️ **Ban Morgana** if we're committing to Comp A (her black shield blanks our engage).
- Ban **Janna / Tahm Kench** if they look like a disengage/peel team.

## Step 3 — Pick a comp (match to what they show)
| Enemy looks like | Run | Key champs to secure |
|------------------|-----|----------------------|
| Unknown / standard | **C Front-to-Back** | Sion, Orianna, Caitlyn |
| Passive / scaling / poke | **B Pick** | Morgana + Nocturne |
| Squishy immobile backline | **D Dive** | Kha'Zix(Shabir) + Geeth ADC |
| Engage / coinflip teamfight | **A Wombo** | 2 of: Malphite/Wukong/Annie + Rell |

## Step 4 — Pick order (general)
1. **Early:** grab contested engage/tanks first — **Malphite / Sion / Rell** (rarely countered, high value). **Maokai** is an uncontested alternative tank (top or jungle).
2. **Middle:** secure the jungle (**Wukong/Viego/Nocturne**, or **Shen/Maokai** for a tank-engage JG) per comp.
3. **Last:** flexible picks — **Annie, Caitlyn, Orianna, LeBlanc** — and let **Geeth pick last** to fill/counter.
4. Always end with **≥2 engage tools** on the board.

## The 4 comps at a glance
**A — Wombo (engage):** Malphite · Wukong · Annie · Caitlyn · Rell → *group, find engage, 5v5 win.*
**B — Pick:** Shen · Nocturne · LeBlanc · Jinx · Morgana → *catch one, take map 5v4.*
**C — Front-to-Back:** Sion · Viego · Orianna · Caitlyn · Alistar → *stand behind tanks, Ori Shockwave.*
**D — Dive:** Malphite · **Kha'Zix(Shabir)** · Diana · **MF(Geeth)** · Rell → *blow up their carry.*

## In-game reminders
- **We win by fighting on our terms** — group mid game, force objectives, don't split or wait.
- Engage from **fog/flanks**, not face-checking into their CC.
- Call targets: focus their **carry or the engaged target**, not the tank.
- Don't throw leads — take Baron/towers after a pick, then end. Mid-game is our window.

## If our core gets banned
- Malphite + Rell gone → **Sion + Alistar + Annie** still gives 3 engages (Comp C).
- Malphite + Sion + Shen all gone → **Maokai** top (engage tank, R is a teamfight root).
- Caitlyn gone → **Ashe** (Shabir) or **Jinx**.

## Tank flex (top or jungle)
- **Maokai:** top OR jungle — engage tank, R roots the whole fight. Fits Wombo / Front-to-Back / Bunker.
- **Shen (JG):** global R for cross-map saves/picks — great in the **Pick** comp. Geeth's best champ (83%).
