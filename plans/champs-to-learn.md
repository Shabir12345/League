# Champs-to-Learn — 1-Month Prep Plan

Goal: by next Clash, everyone is deep on the 2–3 champs that unlock our playbook. **Fewer champs, more reps.** Priorities are ordered; ★ = highest leverage for the team.

## How to practice
- Play your target champs in **soloQ/flex normals**, not just customs — get them comfortable under pressure.
- Run **5-man flex** a few times before Clash to rehearse **Comp A (Wombo)** engages together.
- Track it: aim for ~15–20 games on each #1 priority champ this month.

## ★ Harendra (Top) — HIGHEST TEAM LEVERAGE
Your scattered 30-champ pool is the team's biggest variance. Collapse it to **3 tanks**:
1. **Malphite** ★ — the keystone engage for Comp A. S-tier, low-mechanics, R wins fights. *Make this your one-trick.*
2. **Sion** — S-tier, unkillable frontline + objective soak. Easy. Comp C anchor.
3. **Shen** — global R for cross-map saves (Comp B). Keep as 3rd.

> Drop: Sylas, Ornn (0-4), Volibear (0-4), and the one-off long tail. **3–4 deep > 30 shallow.**
